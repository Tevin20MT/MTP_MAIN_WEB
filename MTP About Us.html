WHO WE ARE

A Company Focused On Smart Solutions

MATSOGO TAU PROJECTS (PTY) LTD is a South African technology-focused company committed to providing practical and reliable solutions for individuals, businesses and organisations.

Our services include CCTV installation, IT support, computer repairs, networking, website development and IT equipment supply.

We believe technology should be accessible, reliable and practical. Our approach is centred around understanding the client's needs and providing solutions that support their goals.
